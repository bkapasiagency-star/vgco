A Surat practice built around the businesses it works with.

Vinay Goenka and Company is a firm of Chartered Accountants based in Vesu, Surat, registered with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India.

The firm works across income tax, GST, TDS, audit and accounting, and supports MSMEs with loan syndication and project finance. Clients deal with one team for their compliance and their financing, which keeps the numbers consistent wherever they are presented.

Plain answers

You are told what is due, what it will take and what the position is, in language you can act on.

Ahead of the date

Filings and audits are planned from the start of the period, so nothing is left to the last week.

One set of numbers

Books, returns, audit and loan proposals are prepared from the same records and reconciled to each other.

Who the firm works with

Most clients are owner managed businesses in and around Surat that want their compliance handled properly and on time.

Private limited companies

Statutory audit, company compliance, income tax and GST through the year.

Partnership and proprietary firms

Books, tax audit, returns and advice on the structure of the business.

MSMEs seeking finance

Project reports, CMA data and loan syndication for term loans and working capital.

Individuals and professionals

Income tax returns, tax planning and replies to notices.
